Task 2: Explain in your own words what is dot product

dot production is a sum of elements multiplicated on each other by their index for example

A = [a1, a2, a3]
B = [b1, b2, b3]

A * B = a1 * b1 + a2 * b2 + a3 * b3

better answer: dot production is multiplication of corresponding elements and the sum of their results


A = [2, 3, 4]
B = [5, 1, 2]

A · B
= 2×5 + 3×1 + 4×2
= 21

Better A · B or dot(A, B) than A * B
